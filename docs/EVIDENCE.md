## What is still on the clock

Settlement is not slow; it is windowed. A true claim opens a 72-hour window that
every other claimant against the same certificate may join, and the whole set is
priced together when it lapses. Nothing below is missing work.

- BoundExceeded (`pnpm run demo:settle`) — certificate #5, claim filed, window closes 2026-08-24 20:55:35 UTC.
- ExpiredCertificate (`pnpm run demo:expiry`) — certificate #20 armed; the late payment cannot prove anything until the 24h grace window ends at 2026-08-23 13:30:19 UTC, and the claim it then files opens its own 72h window.
